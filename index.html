Questions

Do I need to be home?+

No. Most clients leave a key in a lockbox or with concierge. We note access details on your booking and your cleaner locks up when they leave.

Do you bring the supplies?+

Yes. We bring everything, including a vacuum and mop. Our standard products are low-fragrance and plant-based. If you’d prefer we use your own products, leave them out and let us know.

How is the price worked out?+

By service, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and any add-ons. The quote gives you an estimated range. We confirm a fixed price before booking, and it only changes if the home is very different from what was described.

What about pets?+

Pets are welcome. Tell us who lives there when you book, and whether anyone needs to stay in a room or out of the yard while we work.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?+

Give us 24 hours’ notice and there’s no charge. Regular cleans can be paused while you’re away.

What does the bond-back re-clean cover?+

Anything on our end of lease checklist that your agent flags at the final inspection. Send us the agent’s notes within 72 hours and we’ll return to fix it. It doesn’t cover damage, wear, or areas that weren’t accessible on the day.

Who are the cleaners?+

A small team of employed cleaners, not a marketplace of contractors. Each one is police-checked, insured and trained on our checklists before their first clean.
